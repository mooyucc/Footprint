【Description】
Inkfish Footprints is an iOS footprint app designed for travel and life. It helps you light up every place you've been on the world map, storing your travel memories in a personal digital museum. Recording is easy, sharing is beautiful, and memories stay warm.

Core Features
• One-tap check-in with auto-filled location and time, plus smart photo import to instantly organize your albums.
• Multiple beautiful share layouts optimized for popular social platforms, making your posts more ceremonial.
• Journeys unfold as stacked cards, with route maps and statistics, bringing past moments to your fingertips.
• Map supports standard, 3D, and satellite views, staying smooth even with many markers; tap to view details and jump to navigation.
• Smart search with multiple filters like name, region, tag, and favorites to quickly find places from your memory.
• Badge and achievement system that automatically lights up countries and provinces, adding a sense of accomplishment to every trip.

Use Cases
Whether it's a weekend getaway or a long trip, Inkfish Footprints helps you organize your tracks with ease. View multi-day routes by day, and get a clear overview of long holiday footprints.
